01 / THE SHIFT

The bottleneck
is human now.

AI now works on open problems in mathematics and writes production code. Most people still use it as a better search box. A small group gets far more from it, and fewer still earn from it.

The gap is not the model. It is that the model does not know you, and you do not know how to set it up. OnToh gives it the context and setup to work with your life.

The missing layer

Capability is here.
Personal context is not.

02 / RISK

Learn from what he did

That past sale is a clue about Kenta's comfort with losses, not a questionnaire score or a diagnosis.

03 / CLASH

Net opposing orders

If one agent wants to buy what the other wants to sell, the clone nets the orders instead of paying fees twice.

04 / CURRENCY

Show the yen exposure

Both agents think in dollars. The clone translates the combined exposure into the yen Kenta earns and spends.

05 / ACCOUNTS

Match the holding period

The long-hold sleeve goes in NISA. The high-turnover sleeve uses a regular account because a NISA sale does not free its quota until the following year.
